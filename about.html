Who We Are

Africa's Mining Management Platform

Gondwana Mining Management & Services Ltd is one of the emerging and pioneering companies in the field of mining operations management and integrated logistical and technical services in Africa.

Established in Kigali, Rwanda in 2025, the company serves as an administrative and organizational hub for mining projects across the continent — particularly in Sudan, South Sudan, Central African Republic, Chad, Cameroon, Angola, Gabon, and beyond.

The company relies on a team of highly qualified geologists, engineers, and technicians, striving to deliver professional field solutions that ensure maximum efficiency and productivity for every project partner.
