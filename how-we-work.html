How We Work

From concession to production

Every Gondwana project follows a structured, four-phase framework that transforms mining concessions from assessment to full-scale industrial output with full operational management throughout.

I

Assessment & exploration

Two structured site visits to understand the concession in full — from geological character and mineralized veins to security, logistics, water and community context. We prepare a technical report to inform the investment decision.

  • Initial site assessment — access, infrastructure, historical production
  • Direct field testing of samples and mineralized materials
  • Geological mapping and comparison with existing studies
  • Technical report to support investment decision
II

Contracting & legal arrangements

We formalise the partnership through transparent legal structures — defining rights, responsibilities, revenue-sharing, and financial plans before a single machine is mobilised.

  • Partnership and operating agreements
  • Licences, permit and compliance review
  • Revenue-sharing and accountability framework
  • Approved financial and implementation plan
III

Procurement & mobilisation

Equipment, machinery, consumables, and infrastructure are sourced and deployed to site. We manage the full supply chain — including transportation, communications, and storage — across even the most remote African terrain.

  • Purchase or leasing of heavy equipment and machinery
  • Spare parts, fuel, lubricants and processing materials
  • Transportation and communications systems
  • Storage facilities and supporting infrastructure
IV

Site preparation & production

Access roads, settling ponds, processing plants and workshops are constructed. Ore extraction, transportation and processing begin — with waste management maintained to environmental standards throughout.

  • Access road construction and logistical support points
  • Processing plant installation and equipment setup
  • Ore drilling, excavation, loading and haulage
  • Environmental waste management throughout
Ongoing Management

Seven departments. One integrated operation.

Once production begins, Gondwana manages every function in-house — technical, production, logistics, financial, HR, HSE, and external relations.

Technical management

Geological supervision, ore quality monitoring, and periodic technical reporting

Production management

Mining plans, processing plant operations, and production target delivery

Logistics management

Transport supply chains, fuel, spare parts, and equipment movement

Financial management

Revenue tracking, financial reporting, procurement and payments oversight

Human resources

Recruitment, training, performance management, and occupational health

HSE management

Safety procedures, environmental protection, regulatory compliance

Stakeholder relations

Community engagement, government liaison, investor relations

Pilot phase evaluation — built into every project

Every project begins with a structured one-year pilot phase. At its close, Gondwana delivers a comprehensive evaluation covering production volumes, cost per tonne, equipment efficiency, reserve assessment, and a clear recommendation on full commercial scale-up.
