Our Services

Comprehensive mining management and field services — tailored to the realities of African terrain and operations.

Service 01

Mine Management

Comprehensive supervision of mining and production operations, ensuring operational efficiency and maximum output. Our experienced managers oversee every aspect of the mining cycle — from site setup through to production reporting.

We implement modern management systems tailored to each site, ensuring safety standards, productivity targets, and cost controls are met consistently across all conditions.

  • Full site operations management and production supervision
  • Safety compliance and environmental monitoring
  • Workforce coordination and field team management
  • Production reporting and partner communication

Transportation of heavy machinery and equipment to remote sites, with provision of on-site field support across all African terrain types — including desert, jungle, and mountainous environments.

Our logistics capability is one of our most critical differentiators. We have demonstrated the ability to mobilize and sustain operations in conditions that most operators consider inaccessible.

Service 03

Geological Exploration

Geological mapping, geochemical sampling, and mineral resource assessment using advanced surveying and analysis techniques. Our team identifies regions with high mineral potential and provides detailed technical assessments.

Our exploration methodology begins with comprehensive area research, progressing through geochemical sampling, geophysical surveys, and detailed terrain structure analysis to provide bankable data for investment decisions.

  • Detailed geological mapping and terrain analysis
  • Geochemical sampling and mineral indicator analysis
  • Ore reserve assessments and resource estimation
  • Geophysical surveys and structural analysis

Service 04

Integrated Field Services

Supplying production inputs, preparing work sites, maintaining equipment, and providing complete on-the-ground operational support to ensure continuous and efficient mining activity.

We utilize Jacobi Activated Carbon for gold recovery, and the Jinchan Gold Ore Dressing Agent — an eco-friendly alternative to cyanide — for effective leaching while minimising ecological impact.

  • Jacobi Activated Carbon for gold recovery
  • Eco-friendly Jinchan gold leaching agent
  • Site preparation and equipment maintenance
  • Full production input supply and procurement
