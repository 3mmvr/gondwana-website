Community Rights

Local Communities
Are Our True Partners

Gondwana believes that local communities are the real and strategic partners in the success of every mining and development project — not bystanders, but stakeholders with direct and shared interests.

We treat every person whose land holds mineral wealth as a partner in both direct and indirect benefit — not merely an obstacle to economic activity. True development happens when local communities are given the opportunity to participate actively in the economic and investment activities linked to our projects.

Our Approach to Partnership

Gondwana opens the door for local partners to contribute and invest in its projects according to their financial capacities — individually or collectively — while actively encouraging economic and community service initiatives that strengthen local economies and improve quality of life.

Individual Benefits

We prioritise hiring people from local areas, developing their professional skills, and transferring knowledge and expertise to them.

  • ✓ Priority employment for local community members
  • ✓ Professional skills development and training
  • ✓ Knowledge and expertise transfer
  • ✓ Sustainable jobs that improve household incomes

Collective Benefits

We contribute to developing infrastructure and essential services that improve quality of life across communities where we operate.

  • ✓ Schools and educational facilities
  • ✓ Health centres and hospitals
  • ✓ Water networks and road construction
  • ✓ Energy infrastructure and vital installations
