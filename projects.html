Our Methodology

From Exploration to Production

Our exploration process begins with detailed area research aimed at identifying regions with high mineral potential. Through geological mapping, geochemical sampling, and geophysical surveys, our team analyzes terrain structure and mineral indicators with precision.

The transition from exploration to production is managed end-to-end — bringing in the right equipment, people, and processes at each phase to ensure results that meet or exceed partner expectations.

First successful production results were obtained from the Square Waco site — developed in collaboration with our Dubai partners, combining local geological expertise with international experience and capital.
