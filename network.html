Our Offices

Headquarters & Branches

Kigali, Rwanda — HQ

Administrative and organizational headquarters for all African mining operations. Rwanda's stable business environment and central location make it the ideal base for managing projects across the continent. Authorized to operate seven African branches.

Dubai, UAE — Commercial Branch

Administrative and commercial branch facilitating international operations, investor relations, and the global marketing of gold and minerals. Dubai's position as a global commodities hub enables Gondwana to connect African production with international markets.

Sudan & South Sudan — Field Operations

Active field operations across multiple concessions in Sudan and South Sudan — including the Waco, Mirro, and Kilimanjaro Company project areas. These represent Gondwana's most established operational regions with proven production history.

Expansion Markets — Coming Soon

Active exploration and partnership development underway in Chad, Central African Republic, Cameroon, Angola, and Gabon. Geological assessments are ongoing and initial results show strong mineral potential across multiple target areas.

Partnership Model

How We Partner

Gondwana welcomes strategic partnerships with investors, concession holders, and mining companies who share our vision of responsible, efficient, and transparent African mining.

Our Dubai branch serves as the primary point of contact for international investors, while our Kigali HQ coordinates all African field operations, logistics, and technical management.

For Concession Holders

We manage and operate your concession — bringing equipment, expertise, and capital to transform potential into production.

For International Investors

We provide the local expertise, regulatory navigation, and operational capacity to protect and grow your African mining investment.

For Mining Companies

We provide contract management, logistics support, and specialized geological services as an extension of your existing team.
