Cookie Policy

How we use cookies to enhance your experience on our website.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, or phone) when you visit our website. They help us recognize your device on subsequent visits and enhance your user experience.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

These cookies are strictly necessary for the website to function properly. Without these cookies, certain services you request cannot be provided.

  • gondwana-language: Stores your language preference (English or Arabic) so we remember which language you prefer on future visits.

Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors use our website. They collect anonymous information about pages visited, links clicked, and time spent on pages.

Functional Cookies

These cookies enable enhanced functionality and personalization. They remember your preferences and choices to provide a better user experience on repeat visits.

3. Cookie Consent

When you first visit our website, we display a cookie consent notice. By accepting this notice, you consent to our use of cookies as described in this policy. You can withdraw your consent at any time.

4. How to Control Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can:

  • View what cookies are set on your device
  • Delete cookies from your device
  • Block cookies from specific websites
  • Block third-party cookies

Please note that disabling essential cookies may affect the functionality of our website, including your ability to use language preferences.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website may contain links to third-party websites and services. These external sites have their own cookie policies, and we are not responsible for their cookie practices. Please review their privacy policies directly.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect cookie data from unauthorized access and misuse. All cookie data is stored securely and accessed only by authorized personnel.

7. Cookie Policy Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legal requirements, or other factors. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we use cookies.
