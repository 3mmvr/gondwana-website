About Gondwana

A Leader in Africa's Mining Sector

Gondwana Mining Management & Services Ltd is one of the emerging and pioneering companies in mining operations management and integrated logistical and technical services in Africa.

Established in Kigali, Rwanda in 2025, the company serves as an administrative and organizational hub for mining projects across Africa — particularly in Sudan, South Sudan, Central African Republic, Chad, Cameroon, Angola, and Gabon.
